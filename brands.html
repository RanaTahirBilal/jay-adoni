The pattern

Four decades, one craft.

Jay Adoni's career runs through one industry more than any other: shoes and accessories. He founded LJ Simone in 1978, led Pentland USA after its sale in 1993, co founded Pour La Victoire and PLV Studio in 2007, and opened the Brooklyn factory that grew into The Adoni Group and Modern Vice.

Between those companies he worked in the securities industry, developed supermarket sites in Glen Cove, New York, and led the snack company Keep Healthy Inc. His brands have been sold by Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Saks Fifth Avenue, Neiman Marcus and Bergdorf Goodman.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

What brands has Jay Adoni founded?

Jay Adoni founded LJ Simone in 1978 and co founded Pour La Victoire and PLV Studio, home of Kelsi Dagger, in 2007. He also opened the Brooklyn shoe factory that grew into The Adoni Group, whose brands include Modern Vice.

Which stores have sold Jay Adoni's brands?

His brands have been sold by Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Saks Fifth Avenue, Neiman Marcus and Bergdorf Goodman, among others.
