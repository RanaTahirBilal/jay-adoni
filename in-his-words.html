Principles

In his words.

Three lines from his profile in The Dynamos, published in 1987.

01 › On opportunity

It can still happen in this country.

Jay Adoni, quoted in The Dynamos

He said it about his own path: arriving at 17 with $20, and building a manufacturer with hundreds of employees within ten years.

The idea behind his shoe business, as the book described it: notice what is new, produce it quickly, and get it to customers first.

02 › On speed

You got to be able to move fast to notice the new hot styles.

Jay Adoni, quoted in The Dynamos

03 › On work

I don't mind working hard, I never complain.

Jay Adoni, quoted in The Dynamos

Work hard, real hard

Reading the quotes

Three lines, one method.

Read together, the three quotes describe how he built LJ Simone. Believe the opportunity is real. Move faster than the market. Outwork everyone else.

They were spoken in the mid 1980s, when he was 27 and running a manufacturer with more than 250 employees. Four decades and several companies later, the same pattern runs through every venture on this site.

In his family's words

The story, as his sons tell it.

His sons, who relaunched the Modern Vice shoe brand in New York, have told the press where it all began: their father started out taking out the garbage at a cousin's shoe factory, learned to run the machines, and later opened a factory of his own.

“Our father inspired us to do this,” one of them told Fashion Week Daily. The other told Fashionista that he grew up, quite literally, around piles of shoes.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

What is Jay Adoni's best known quote?

“It can still happen in this country.” He said it in the 1980s about his own path from arriving with $20 to running a large shoe manufacturer.

Where do the Jay Adoni quotes come from?

The three quotes come from his profile in Brett Kingstone's book The Dynamos: Who Are They, Anyway?, which described his company in the mid 1980s.

What is Jay Adoni's approach to business?

In his own words: believe the opportunity is real, move fast to catch new styles, and do not mind working hard.
