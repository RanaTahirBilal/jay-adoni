He arrived with $20.
He built brands America wore.

Jacob Adoni, known as Jay Adoni, arrived in the United States from Israel in 1976, aged 17, hardly speaking English. Two years later he founded LJ Simone, one of the most successful American fashion accessories brands of the 1980s.

Quick answers

Who is Jay Adoni?

Who is Jay Adoni?

Jacob Adoni, known as Jay Adoni, is an entrepreneur who arrived in the United States from Israel in 1976, aged 17, with $20. He founded the fashion accessories brand LJ Simone in 1978 and PLV Studio in 2007, and developed commercial real estate in Glen Cove, New York.

What does Jay Adoni do today?

Today Jay Adoni works in his shoe factory in Brooklyn, New York, and runs a rare coin business.

What companies has Jay Adoni founded?

LJ Simone, a handbag and shoe brand registered to his company Admos Shoe Corp., and PLV Studio, Inc., maker of Pour La Victoire and Kelsi Dagger. He is also the sole member of Harbor Park Realty, LLC.

How big did LJ Simone grow?

He built LJ Simone to over $120 million in annual sales. Pentland Brands acquired it in 1993 and he became President of Pentland USA.

What has Jay Adoni done in real estate?

As a licensed Real Estate Broker he developed a ShopRite site in Glen Cove, New York, managed its sale to Ahold, developed a supermarket sold to King Kullen and built a Walgreens anchored shopping center.

Who has Jay Adoni mentored?

Jay Adoni hired Steve Madden as a sales rep at L.J. Simone, and WWD’s Footwear News Hall of Fame profile later named Adoni and Lance Rubin among Madden’s early mentors.

Where has Jay Adoni been covered in the press?

WWD has quoted him as co founder and chief executive of Pour La Victoire, and The New York Times quoted him in 2002 during the Glen Cove supermarket development. See all press.

Is Jay Adoni the same person as Jacob Adoni?

Yes. Jacob Adoni is his legal name and Jay is the name he is known by.
