Modern Vice.

A shoe factory in Brooklyn, a family business, and shoes handmade in New York City.

The Adoni Group

It started with a factory.

After arriving in the 1970s, he opened a shoe factory in Brooklyn. It grew into The Adoni Group, a family shoe business, and Modern Vice became one of its brands.

His sons later relaunched Modern Vice, with shoes handmade in New York City.

Investor, director, adviser.

  1. 2012 to 2014

    TAG, Inc.

  2. Since 2015

The brand

Made in New York City.

Modern Vice launched in 2012 as a made to order brand, producing its shoes in New York City. It began with men's sneakers and grew into a full line of men's and women's boots, including the Jett bootie, moto boots and Chelsea boots. Every style is named after a rock star.

The shoes are made in a glass walled factory and showroom in Manhattan's Garment District, by a team of about ten shoemakers working with leather from American and Italian tanneries. In its first year the factory created more than 40 jobs. The same workshop also makes ice skates, after taking over the business of a veteran Queens skate maker.

In 2013, Inc. Magazine reported that Jay Adoni served as CEO of The Adoni Group and called him a fashion industry elder statesman. The Adoni Group, the company that grew out of the Brooklyn factory, describes itself as a multi branded shoes, handbags and accessories company selling to luxury and department stores, boutiques and national chains.

Who wears it

On stage and on screen.

Modern Vice has been worn by Beyoncé, Whoopi Goldberg, Lauryn Hill, Rita Ora and Bella Thorne, and appeared on the television series Love.

At the 2022 Grammy Awards, Lenny Kravitz wore custom knee high Modern Vice boots made in collaboration with Church Boutique. The factory has also produced shoe lines for other fashion labels.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

What is Jay Adoni's connection to Modern Vice?

Jay Adoni was an investor and director of TAG, Inc., home of Modern Vice, from 2012 to 2014, and has consulted for Modern Vice since 2015.

Where does Jay Adoni work today?

Today Jay Adoni works in his shoe factory in Brooklyn, New York, and runs a rare coin business.

Where are Modern Vice shoes made?

Modern Vice shoes are made in New York City, in a factory and showroom in Manhattan's Garment District.

Who has worn Modern Vice?

Modern Vice has been worn by Beyoncé, Whoopi Goldberg, Lauryn Hill, Rita Ora and Bella Thorne, and Lenny Kravitz wore custom Modern Vice boots at the 2022 Grammy Awards.
