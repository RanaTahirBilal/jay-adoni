Modern Vice.

A shoe factory in Brooklyn, a family business, and shoes handmade in New York City.

The Adoni Group

It started with a factory.

After arriving in the 1970s, he opened a shoe factory in Brooklyn. It grew into The Adoni Group, a family shoe business, and Modern Vice became one of its brands.

His sons later relaunched Modern Vice, with shoes handmade in New York City.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

What is Jay Adoni's connection to Modern Vice?

Jay Adoni was an investor and director of TAG, Inc., home of Modern Vice, from 2012 to 2014, and has consulted for Modern Vice since 2015.

Where does Jay Adoni work today?

Today Jay Adoni works in his shoe factory in Brooklyn, New York, and runs a rare coin business.
