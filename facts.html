Frequently asked

Questions about Jay Adoni.

Who is Jay Adoni?

Jacob Adoni, known as Jay Adoni, is a New York entrepreneur. He arrived in the United States from Israel in 1976, aged 17, founded LJ Simone in 1978 and PLV Studio in 2007, served as President of Pentland USA, and developed commercial real estate in Glen Cove, New York.

Is Jay Adoni the same person as Jacob Adoni?

Yes. Jacob Adoni is his legal name and Jay is the name he is known by.

What is Jay Adoni doing now?

Today Jay Adoni works in his shoe factory in Brooklyn, New York, and runs a rare coin business.

What brands is Jay Adoni known for?

LJ Simone, which he founded in 1978, and the Pour La Victoire and Kelsi Dagger brands of PLV Studio, which he founded in 2007. He has also been an investor, director and consultant for Modern Vice.

Where can I contact Jay Adoni?

Through the contact form at jayadoni.com/contact.html, for partnerships, business and press.
