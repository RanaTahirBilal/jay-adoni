Quick answers

Questions, answered.

Is Jay Adoni a real estate broker?

Yes. Jay Adoni is a licensed real estate broker and the sole member of Harbor Park Realty, LLC, a real estate development and management company.

What did Jay Adoni develop in Glen Cove?

He developed a supermarket site in Glen Cove, New York in 1995 and managed its sale to the Ahold group in 2001. He also developed a supermarket sold to King Kullen and a shopping center anchored by Walgreens.

What is Harbor Park Realty?

Harbor Park Realty, LLC is Jay Adoni's real estate development and management company, of which he is the sole member.

Which supermarkets did Jay Adoni's Glen Cove sites serve?

His Glen Cove developments included a ShopRite supermarket site, later sold to the Ahold group, owner of Stop & Shop, and a new supermarket sold to King Kullen.
