Keep Healthy.

After four decades in fashion, finance and real estate, a new category: snack food.

Chief Executive Officer

A fast growing snack company.

He has served as CEO of Keep Healthy Inc, a rapidly growing snack food company.

The company filed the KEEP HEALTHY trademark in 2014 for dried fruit snacks, fruit based bars and raw food bars.

KEEP HEALTHY2014

Trademark goods

  • Dried fruit snacks
  • Fruit based bars
  • Raw food bars

The company

Made on Long Island.

Keep Healthy Inc is based in Northport, on the North Shore of Long Island, New York. It makes snack bars built around fruit, nuts and superfoods, offered as a healthy alternative to processed, sugary snacks.

Every product is made in the USA. The fruit and nut bars use only a handful of ingredients, about three on average, and the range is labeled vegan, kosher, gluten free and non GMO.

The bars are sold through the company's own store at keephealthyinc.com and have been listed on Walmart.com.

The range

Three lines of bars.

Fruit and Nut Snack Bars: Mango Almond, Pineapple Coconut, Apple Walnut Date, Cashew Date and Blueberry Pecan.

Chocolate Covered Fruit and Nut Bars: Blueberry, Cherry, Mango Almond and Pineapple Coconut.

Organic Low Glycemic Protein Bars: Sea Salt Brownie, Chocolate Fudge, Chocolate Coconut, Banana Nut and Apple Cinnamon. Variety packs and larger fruit and nut platters round out the line.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

What does Keep Healthy Inc make?

Keep Healthy Inc makes fruit and nut snack bars, chocolate covered fruit and nut bars and organic low glycemic protein bars, all made in the USA.

Where is Keep Healthy based?

Keep Healthy Inc is based in Northport, Long Island, New York.

What is Jay Adoni's role at Keep Healthy?

Jay Adoni has served as Chief Executive Officer of Keep Healthy Inc, the snack food company behind the KEEP HEALTHY brand.
