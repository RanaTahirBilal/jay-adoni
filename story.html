Chapter one › The journey

Twenty dollars, and no English.

In 1976, Jacob "Jay" Adoni, a 17 year old from Israel, arrived in America with nothing more than his ambition and a twenty dollar bill.

1976

The arrival

He hardly spoke English. What he had was the will to work hard, real hard.

The book that later profiled him would call his rise something that seems more like a story about the immigrants of a century ago.

The record

How the story kept going.

  1. 1978

    Founds LJ Simone

    He decided it was time to start his own business. LJ Simone became one of the most successful American fashion accessories brands of the 1980s, growing to over $120 million in annual sales.

  2. 1987

    Profiled in The Dynamos

    Brett Kingstone's book profiled him as the owner of a shoe manufacturing company, Admos Shoe Corporation: over 250 employees, over $20 million in sales that year, and customers including Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's and Macy's.

  3. 1993

    Sells LJ Simone to Pentland Brands

    The global fashion accessories company acquired LJ Simone, and he served as President of Pentland USA.

  4. 1993 to 2007

    Business and real estate

    He engaged in various businesses and became a licensed Real Estate Broker, developing commercial real estate in Glen Cove, New York.

  5. 2007

    Founds PLV Studio

    A women's shoes and accessories maker and retailer of two global brands, Pour La Victoire and Kelsi Dagger. VMG Partners acquired a majority interest in 2014.

  6. 2012 onward

    Investor, director, adviser and CEO

    Investor and director of TAG, Inc., home of Modern Vice, then a consultant to Modern Vice. He has also served as CEO of Keep Healthy Inc, a fast growing snack food company.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

Where is Jay Adoni from?

Jacob Adoni, known as Jay Adoni, arrived in the United States from Israel in 1976, aged 17.

What jobs did Jay Adoni have before starting a business?

He worked as a bus boy, a cook, a painter, a warehouseman and a shipping clerk in a shoe factory before founding LJ Simone in 1978.

Is Jay Adoni the same person as Jacob Adoni?

Yes. Jacob Adoni is his legal name and Jay is the name he is known by.
