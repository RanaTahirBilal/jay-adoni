Ventures › 1978 to 1993

LJ Simone.

Founded when he was 19. Built into one of the most successful American fashion accessories brands of the 1980s.

The start

Two years in America.

After busing tables, cooking, painting and working in warehouses, he decided it was time to start his own business. In 1978 he founded LJ Simone.

The brand covered ladies' handbags and shoes for men and women, registered to his company, Admos Shoe Corp. of New York.

The climb

From a stockroom to a national brand.

He built LJ Simone from its creation to over $120 million in annual sales. In 1993 the global fashion accessories company Pentland Brands acquired it, and he became President of Pentland USA.

A future star on the sales floor

Where Steve Madden learned the trade.

One of LJ Simone's early hires was a young salesman named Steve Madden. He joined around 1980 and stayed for roughly nine years, selling and designing styles for the teen market, such as fringe boots and penny loafers, before launching his own company in 1990.

Jay Adoni ran the company alongside Lance Rubin. In 2019, WWD’s Footwear News Hall of Fame profile of Steve Madden named the two of them among Madden’s early mentors. Over the decade the business added more marks: L.J. SIMONE for sneakers and shoes, VIDEO BY L.J. SIMONE for handbags, and VERA CRUZ for women's shoes. Its shoes were sold to Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Bullock's and Dayton Hudson.

Quick answers

Questions, answered.

Who founded LJ Simone?

Jay Adoni (Jacob Adoni) founded LJ Simone in 1978, when he was 19. The L. J. SIMONE trademark for handbags and shoes was registered to his company, Admos Shoe Corp. of New York.

What happened to LJ Simone?

Pentland Brands acquired LJ Simone in 1993, and Jay Adoni became President of Pentland USA.

Did Steve Madden work at LJ Simone?

Yes. Steve Madden worked at LJ Simone for about nine years, starting around 1980 as a salesman, before founding his own shoe company in 1990.

Which stores sold LJ Simone?

In the 1980s its shoes were sold to Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Bullock's and Dayton Hudson.
