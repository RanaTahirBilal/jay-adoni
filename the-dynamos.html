The Dynamos.

Brett Kingstone's book on America's top entrepreneurs under 30 gave him a chapter of his own: Jay Adoni of Admos Shoe Corporation.

THE
DYNAMOSWho Are They, Anyway?Brett Kingstone

Photo from The Dynamos by Brett Kingstone, 1986, restored with AI

What the book recorded

At 27, already a manufacturer.

While others called the American shoe industry beyond saving, he was expanding.

Admos Shoe Corporation

Employees

Buying while others closed

As competitors shut their factories, he expanded, buying their equipment and inventory at liquidation sales.

Always something new

His secret, the book said, was constantly offering customers an array of new styles. He and his staff travelled to Europe often to stay on top of fashion.
